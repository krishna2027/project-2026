How to Use

👋

Show your sign

Hold your hand up clearly in front of the camera so MediaPipe can detect all 21 landmarks.

⏱

Hold steady (~1 s)

Keep the sign stable until the Stability bar fills completely. The letter is then typed automatically.

📝

See it typed

Every accepted sign appears in the text panel on the right. Use the controls below to add spaces or correct mistakes.

🔊

Speak aloud

Press Speak to read your composed message out loud using the browser's text-to-speech engine.

⚠️

Model not yet trained

The sign recognition model has not been trained yet. Camera hand-tracking is fully working – you can watch the landmark skeleton live. To enable automatic sign recognition, follow the steps in the README.md:

  1. Run python scripts/collect_data.py to record your training samples.
  2. Run python scripts/train_model.py to train and save the model.
  3. Restart the server – the model loads automatically on startup.
